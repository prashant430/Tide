With their ancient histories, minimalist architecture and tastes of the sea, the Greek Islands are a sailing paradise – and the theme of our latest issue. We reveal the fresh spots that are exciting locals as much as summer visitors, from a run of recently launched eco-minded hotels to sweet new tavernas with heart. And we share the ultimate route to take around the northern Dodecanese, filled with secret bays and tucked-away anchorages, on what remains a frontier unexplored by most sailors. Elsewhere, we meet with the owner and Captain of Kiboko 4, the newly crowned Superyacht of the Year that skims across our cover, to discuss her 'Beautiful Lightness of Being' . And we explore second lives: that of the 78ft Drumfire, the boat reborn in Australia following many quiet years in the Med; and that of Ravenger – Refit of the Year – and sleek performance cruiser Samurai – plus much, much more.

For this edition we go on a South American adventure, joining Aragon’s crew after their 21-day crossing of the Southern Ocean through the Roaring Forties and Screaming Fifties to sail Patagonia’s glacial waters and fjords. We then retreat into a place with front-row seats to the region’s wild landscapes, and onto Uruguay’s José Ignacio for a delicate Pinot Rosé at its local winery. In Panama, the remote, untamed Islas Secas beckon, followed by Caribbean waters and a private island tucked within Colombia’s Rosario archipelago – all rounded off by a route along Cuba’s south-eastern coastline. One Superyacht we catch up with before she too moves onto South America is the sublime, award-winning Sarissa, while back on the Med Black Pearl demonstrates the powerful potential of the DynaRig system – plus much, much more.

All eyes look North for this year’s Winter issue, to explore the thousands of isles that make up Scandinavia, land of mystic lights and days filled with epic sailing adventures. From Spirit match racing in Stockholm to an itinerary hopping between secret gems in the Swedish archipelago, onto a secluded eco-cabin on the tiny Finnish island of Skjulskäre, we let the region’s magic wash over us. We also explore Superyacht Nilaya with her Captain, sit down with Southern Spas and Doyle Sails to discuss the pioneering ways they’re both harnessing ‘wind as power’, and set sail for the Pacific archipelago to visit nature-first Bawah Reserve. All this before we move back to Antigua to hide away for the rest of the end-of-year season, this time at the revived and reopened Hermitage Bay – plus much, much more.

For TIDE’s special tenth issue, it’s all eyes on Italy – specifically the Tuscan Archipelago, an ancient set of rugged, romantic isles set between vineyard and sea. We explore Pianosa, a former prison island for Mafia bosses which has become a beautiful example of rewilding, Elba, a gritty isle indelibly associated with Napoleon yet with so much more to her story, plus a string of smaller spots full of surprises in our La Dolce Vita Itinerary. Out on the water, we delve into a mighty trio of at once rapid and sleek boats and brands: from new ocean gladiator Maximus and the legendary Kokomo to the fast-accelerating Gunboat brand, before circling back and picking out our ten favourite destinations sailed to so far in honour of our double-digit edition – plus much, much more.

Inspired by the 37th America’s Cup, TIDE’s ninth issue celebrates the boats that helped build the prestigious contest – from meeting a Captain with 10 years’ experience sailing J Class veteran Velsheda, to remembering the Twelve Metre heroes, still the longest-running Class in yachting history. Fast-forward 173 years, and we hone in on the latest pinnacle of design: the first ever racing Superyacht, Raven. Her Captain, designer and Sailing Manager all share with us how she came to be. And while the world’s best sailors battle it out on the water, we invite you to explore Barcelona’s new wave of Catalonian cuisine, before sailing to the nearby Balearics with our sailing itinerary for each island – plus much, much more.
